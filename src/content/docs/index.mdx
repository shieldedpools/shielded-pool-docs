## Why use it

NIGHT is Midnight’s unshielded token: every balance and every transfer is public. sNIGHT is the same value as a **shielded** token. Once you hold it, what you own and who you pay are not visible on chain.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Private once inside" icon="seti:lock">
    sNIGHT moves as ordinary Midnight shielded coins. Transfers hide the sender, recipient, amount, and token.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Always 1:1, fully backed" icon="approve-check">
    Every sNIGHT is minted in the same step that locks one NIGHT. The locked balance is public, so anyone can check it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nobody can change it" icon="setting">
    The contract was deployed with no admin key. Nobody can upgrade it, pause it, or take the NIGHT backing your sNIGHT.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No protocol fee" icon="star">
    The contract keeps none of your NIGHT. You pay only Midnight’s normal network fee, in DUST.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
